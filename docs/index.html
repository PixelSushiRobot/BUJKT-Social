BUJKT Social

A Chrome extension that makes Objkt Social smoother. Notifications, messages and price-sorted bookmarks sit in one column, so you can catch offers and new drops without leaving the feed.

Download latest GitHub

For Chrome and other Chromium browsers. Not on the Chrome Web Store.

Install

  1. Unzip bujkt-social.zip.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.

To update, swap in the new folder and press reload.

What it does

  • Social nav in the left rail, with unread badges and ⌘K search.
  • Notifications regrouped, with open offers easy to spot.
  • Bookmarks loaded in full and sorted by price.
  • Messages with the chat list and chat side by side.
  • For you: stats, offers, auctions ending soon and new drops.

Unofficial, personal-use tool. Not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by objkt AG or the OBJKT Marketplace. "BUJKT Social" is fan wordplay, not the objkt or Objkt Social brand.

It changes how objkt's pages look and behave, loads objkt pages in hidden panels, and asks objkt's public data service and TzKT for marketplace data. objkt's Terms and Conditions restrict extensions that modify their pages without authorization, so this tool may not be permitted.
